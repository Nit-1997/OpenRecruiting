# Why and what we open-sourced

> Outline. The full post is a follow-on writing effort.

- What the platform does: AI intake, candidate/round management, meeting capture,
  AI interview feedback, a recruiting knowledge graph.
- Why open-source it rather than archive it: the work is worth more read than
  deleted, and HR-tech builders can fork it.
- What "archived reference release" honestly means, and what it does not.
- The bar set before publishing: safe (no secrets, no customer data, known
  security issues handled), honest (nothing over-promised), clean (no trace of
  the old brand).
- The shape of the job: nine phases, each leaving a stack that comes up.
